Skill · Grill Me by Matt Pocock

The hard part isn't the model. It's shared understanding.

Claude interviews you, round by round, until you both see the same task. Then it builds.

Try it

claude plugin install mattpocock-skills@claude-plugins-official
/grill-me + your task in one sentence
github.com/mattpocock/skills → grill-me

Example

"/grill-me Every week I want a status report for the steering committee, built from the project export in this folder."

❓ Q1 · Audience: Details, or only what needs a decision? ➡️ Recommended: decisions and risks only.
❓ Q2 · Length: One page per week? ➡️ Recommended: yes, in your existing template.
❓ Q3 · Traffic light: When is a milestone red? ➡️ Recommended: more than two weeks late.

Your answers open new questions:
❓ Q4 · Escalation: Name an owner and a proposal for every red milestone? ➡️ Recommended: yes.
❓ Q5 · Trend: Show the change since last week? ➡️ Recommended: yes, one arrow per work package.

Claude sums up what's decided (audience, length, traffic light, escalation, trend) and asks: "Is that right? Then I'll start." Only now does it build. Five minutes of questions save three rounds of rework.
