Principle 4 · Write the right prompt

Brief it like a new colleague.

What?

The task, in full sentences.

For whom?

Who reads or uses the result.

What form?

Length, format, template.

Done when?

What "good" looks like.

Not: "Make a status report."
But: "One-page status report for the steering committee from the export in this folder. Decisions and risks only, red = more than two weeks late."
